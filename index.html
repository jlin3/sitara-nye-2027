Getting There

Three ways in

Via Montevideo

MVD — the main gateway. Overnight flights from the US via MIA/PTY, then a scenic 2.5-hour drive up the coast past Punta del Este. Transfers arranged for arrival waves.

Via Buenos Aires

EZE → PDP — fly to Buenos Aires, then a 50-minute hop to Punta del Este's airport, about 40 minutes from the estate. Great option if you want a BA night first.

The showoff route

By helicopter — the estate has its own helipad. Charter from Punta or Montevideo and land on the lawn. Somebody should do it so the rest of us can watch.
